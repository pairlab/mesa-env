# Fixtures

## MESA Fixtures

We use the following fixtures in our tasks. Each one has hundreds of variants generated by iterating over the textures available in the `assets` folder.

 - `microwave`, a microwave
 - `cabinet`, a cabinet with three drawers
 - `slide_cabinet`, a cabinet with a door that slides horizontally
 - `sliding_top_box`, a box with a top that slides horizontally. This box rotates freely, necessitating pusing motions in all directions to open and close

## Included Fixtures

Our framework supports a variety of fixtures that we decided not to include in the MESA tasks, but are fully supported by our task generation pipeline. Please refer to the `mesa/sim/envs/objects` file for the full list.
